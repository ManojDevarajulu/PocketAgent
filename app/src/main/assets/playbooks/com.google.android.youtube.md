Use this to find and play videos. Search inside YouTube, not in a browser.

1. The magnifier labelled "Search" is at the top right. The bottom tabs are Home, Shorts, Create and You.
   A mini player with a progress bar may sit above the tabs while a video plays.
2. Tap the magnifier. The search field shows "Search YouTube" and a list of suggestions appears below it.
3. Type the words, then tap the matching suggestion or press the search key on the keyboard.
4. Results are in a scrollable list. Tap the video row to play it.
5. Do not tap "Voice search" or "Create".
