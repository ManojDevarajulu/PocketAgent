Always search in a new tab. Do not reuse the tab that is already open, and do not type over its address bar.

1. Open a new tab first: tap the three-dot menu at the top right and choose "New tab". If the menu is not
   visible, tap the tab-count button in the toolbar and then the "+" (new tab) button.
2. In the new tab, tap the address bar, type the search words, and press the keyboard's search/Go key.
3. Do this for every new search, even if the previous search is still open.
