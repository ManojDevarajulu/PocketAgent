Use this for email. Each email row in the inbox shows the sender, subject and a snippet.

1. The inbox search box at the top is labelled "Ask Gmail". If it is not visible, scroll up. Open an email
   by tapping its row.
2. Inside an email, the bar at the top has Archive, Delete and "Mark as unread". "Reply" and "Forward"
   are buttons at the bottom of the email. Delete and send always need the user's yes.
3. To write a new email, tap the "Compose" button at the bottom right. The fields are To, Subject and
   "Compose email" (the body). Tap To and type the address, then tap its suggestion. The "Send" button is in
   the top bar. Ignore "Help me write" and "Gemini".
4. If the keyboard is open, Back closes the keyboard first and a second Back leaves the screen. An empty
   draft is thrown away.
